## Summary

Add a root `EDITORIAL.md` that captures the house style, tone, and structural conventions for posts on "()=>{} | Void's blog", modelled on the sibling `../kaplus/EDITORIAL.md` but adapted to this repository's realities: Jekyll/kramdown Markdown (not AsciiDoc), a single-language English site (not the PL/EN bilingual periodical), YAML front-matter and topic sections (not print page-count slots), and web publication (not a magazine `.docx` build). The guidelines describe the *editorial* layer — scope and audience, front-matter contract, structure, images, code, links, and the author's voice — while `CLAUDE.md` keeps owning the *build/architecture* layer.

The document is grounded in the blog's actual expression (the recent `genai/` and `arch/` posts) and deliberately treats the recent posts as the **living exemplars** of voice, so it stays consistent with the `english-correction` skill, which samples the corpus on every run rather than following a frozen rulebook. The plan also wires the new file into the existing authoring skills and `CLAUDE.md` so the guidelines are a referenced source of truth, not orphaned documentation. Because it touches `CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/**`, the change is logged via the `claude-meta-changelog` skill.

## Rationale

The kaplus repository has an `EDITORIAL.md` that gives its articles a single, citable statement of house style — length budgets, title/byline form, bilingual practice, and the author's register — separate from the layout mechanics in its `CLAUDE.md`. This blog has no equivalent. Its conventions currently live only implicitly in the posts themselves and partly inside the `english-correction` skill (British-English rules, single-quote emphasis, `###` headings, reflective first-person tone). That works for the correction skill, but there is no human-readable editorial charter an author can read before drafting, and no canonical anchor the skills can point at.

Adding `EDITORIAL.md` gives new and revised posts a consistent target for style, tone, and structure, and gives the `english-correction` and `adversarial-challenge` skills a named reference for what "on-tone" and "honours its opening promise" mean here. The kaplus file is the template for *form and spirit*, not content: this blog does not have page-count slots, a bilingual contract, an AsciiDoc header/byline convention, or an `out/` build, so those sections are replaced by their Jekyll/web equivalents (YAML front-matter, `categories`, `description`, `pic`, the `permalink: /:title` scheme, section directories, the `_drafts/` → `_posts/` lifecycle).

Two design decisions, confirmed with the author:

- **Placement + skill wiring.** The guidelines live in a root `EDITORIAL.md` mirroring kaplus, and the existing skills plus `CLAUDE.md` are updated to reference it, so the guidelines are enforceable rather than decorative.
- **Describe voice, defer to the corpus.** The voice section characterises the register (reflective first-person essay, wry asides, concrete recollection, British English) but explicitly names the recent `genai/`/`arch` posts as the living exemplars, so it does not fossilise against `english-correction`'s deliberate corpus-sampling.

The guidelines describe the canonical form for **new and revised** posts; they do not oblige a retro-edit of existing posts, matching the "known deviation, not a defect" stance kaplus takes.

  - **Answer:** No fixed length budget. It may add the standing guidance that the post should be as long as it needs to be and no longer — but it must not impose a numeric slot.
- [x] **Q4.** Should the `adversarial-challenge` skill's "contract" (front-matter + opening thesis + optional length budget) be formalised in `EDITORIAL.md` as the author-facing definition of a post's promise, and the skill updated to reference it — or should `EDITORIAL.md` merely describe the opening-promise expectation without changing the skill's contract wording?
  - **Answer:** Yes — formalise the contract. `EDITORIAL.md` defines a post's **contract** as the author-facing promise the piece must honour: its front-matter (`title`, `description`, `categories`) plus the thesis/promise stated in its opening paragraphs. Consistent with Q3, the contract carries **no length-budget component** (there is no numeric slot on this site). `adversarial-challenge` is updated to hold articles against that `EDITORIAL.md` definition of the contract rather than an inline restatement.
