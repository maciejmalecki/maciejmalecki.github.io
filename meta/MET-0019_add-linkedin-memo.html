Summary

Add the linkedin-memo skill and extend task-utils and CLAUDE.md so published posts can carry an optional, build-excluded LinkedIn summary/advertisement memo cross-linked via the post’s task (executes plan-005).

Purpose

To let selected published blog posts carry an optional, ready-to-paste LinkedIn promotion (summary + advertisement + back-link to the live article) without polluting the published site or the four-gate post-authoring flow — realised as the linkedin-memo skill writing build-excluded memo files, with task-utils and CLAUDE.md extended to record and validate the memo’s cross-link to its post. This executes plan-005.
